About Our Institute

Welcome to KCNIT Group of Institutions, Banda (Uttar Pradesh). We are committed to providing quality technical and professional education to empower future engineers and managers.

Departmental Information

1. Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)

Focuses on modern computer science disciplines including programming, web development, data structures, and artificial intelligence.

Key Features:

  • Modern Computer Labs with high-speed internet
  • Hands-on training in Web Development & Software Engineering

2. Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME)

Deals with the design, analysis, manufacturing, and maintenance of mechanical systems.

Key Features:

  • Fully equipped Workships and Thermodynamics Labs
  • Focus on CAD/CAM software and practical design

3. Department of Civil Engineering (CE)

Prepares students for planning, designing, and constructing infrastructure projects like roads, bridges, and buildings.

Key Features:

  • Surveying and Material Testing Labs
  • On-site industrial visits and practical field training

4. Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE)

Covers electrical machines, power systems, control systems, and electronic circuit designs.

Key Features:

  • Advanced Electrical Machines Lab
  • Projects on renewable energy and circuit design
